# Code of conduct

This project is a place to do careful work with people you have not met. The
standard is simple: be someone others can disagree with productively.

## Expected

* **Critique the code, not the person.** "This will panic on an empty vault" is
  useful; "you clearly didn't think" is not.
* **Assume competence, ask before concluding.** Most bad-looking decisions had a
  constraint you cannot see. Ask what it was.
* **Be specific.** A bug report with steps and a version is a gift. A verdict is
  not.
* **Accept that maintainers say no.** Scope decisions are not judgements of your
  work. A rejection with a reason is respect, not dismissal.
* **Give credit.** If someone's idea, report or patch helped, say so.
* **Respect privacy.** Do not publish another person's credentials, logs, or
  private repository contents — including in a bug report. Redact first.

## Not acceptable

* Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
* Personal, political or identity-based attacks; slurs of any kind.
* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any sort.
* Publishing private information without explicit permission.
* Deliberately wasting reviewers' time: bad-faith pull requests, sealioning,
  or reopening a settled decision to relitigate it.
* Using project spaces to sell something unrelated.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, code
comments, and anywhere you are representing the project — including conferences
and social media when you are speaking as a contributor.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the Astrivya organisation
on GitHub (https://github.com/Astrivya) — a direct message to a maintainer is
enough to start. If the report concerns a maintainer, say so; that person will
not be part of handling it.

Include what happened, where, when, and who was involved. Screenshots help.
You will get an acknowledgement within **3 business days**. Your report and
identity stay private.

## Enforcement

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| A single sharp comment, a bad day | A private note; no record |
| Repeated dismissiveness after being asked to stop | Warning, and the thread is locked if needed |
| Harassment, slurs, intimidation | Immediate temporary ban from project spaces |
| Publishing private information, or retaliation for a report | Permanent ban |
| Anything involving a credible threat of harm | Permanent ban and a report to the platform |

Enforcement is decided by maintainers who were not involved in the incident,
using the smallest response that stops the behaviour. Bans state their duration
and what would end them. Appeals go to the same channel and are answered once.

We will not discuss a report's details with the person who was reported beyond
what is needed to act on it.
