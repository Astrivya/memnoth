# Trademark policy

The Apache License, Version 2.0 grants you copyright and patent rights to the
code. It does **not** grant trademark rights (Apache-2.0, section 6). This file
explains what that means in practice.

## What is covered

The names **memnoth**, **mn**, **Astrivya**, the memnoth logo, and the visual
identity of the project (the "Marks"). These are trademarks of Astrivya.

## What you may do without asking

* **Refer to the project.** "Built on memnoth", "compatible with memnoth",
  "a memnoth plugin", "forked from memnoth" — nominative, factual references
  are fine, including in articles, talks, package descriptions and comparison
  pages.
* **Use the name in a fork's documentation** to state what the fork is derived
  from, e.g. "acme-tui, based on memnoth".
* **Redistribute unmodified binaries** you built from this source, under the
  name memnoth, provided you ship LICENSE and NOTICE unmodified.
* **Ship packages that depend on memnoth** and name it as a dependency.

## What you may not do without written permission

* **Publish a modified build under the memnoth or mn name.** If you change the
  code, distribute it under your own name — a patched binary calling itself
  memnoth is the one thing that makes bug reports and security advisories
  impossible to route.
* **Use the Marks in a product name, company name, domain name, or app-store
  listing** in a way that suggests endorsement or that your product *is*
  memnoth.
* **Use the logo** in your own product's iconography, or as the primary mark of
  a fork.
* **Imply sponsorship, certification, or affiliation** that does not exist.

## Forks

You may fork freely under Apache-2.0. If you distribute a modified version:

1. Rename it. Pick a name that is not confusingly similar to memnoth or mn.
2. Replace the logo and the `memnoth` / `mn` binary names in your packaging.
3. Keep the copyright notices and NOTICE attribution for the code you received.
4. State clearly in your README that your project is a fork and is not
   affiliated with or supported by Astrivya.

The license gives you the code; it does not give you the identity.

## Reporting misuse

Open an issue at https://github.com/Astrivya/memnoth or contact the maintainers
through the Astrivya organisation. We will always talk first; this policy exists
to prevent confusion, not to police commentary, criticism, or comparison.
